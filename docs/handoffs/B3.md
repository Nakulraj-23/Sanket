- Variants (val macro-F1): words 0.8672; words+trigrams d64 0.8820 (chosen); d128 0.8759. `make train-nn` 57 s wall.
- Torch vs ONNX parity tested on 200 val texts under 1e-4; `artifacts/` total 9.7 MB (includes B2's files).
- The NN loses by 1.4 macro-F1 points (0.5 on team accuracy) but is about 125x faster per ticket. Likely reason: a mean of
  embeddings ignores word order and has 77 classes with about 90 rows each, while the baseline's TF-IDF on words and characters
  is a strong fit at this size. Not tuned further. Linear model keeps serving. Baseline latency (7.7 ms) is mostly TF-IDF feature work.

## Drift
- `ks_2samp` equals scipy (D to 1e-9, p within 1e-3 on 5 seeded cases incl. ties): exact p-value via lattice paths for n*m up to 1e6, asymptotic series beyond.
- `psi(expected, actual, bins)`: bins = count (reference quantiles) or edge array; floor 1e-4. `psi_from_shares` for team mix.
- `drift_report(reference, live, model_version="v1")` returns B0's DriftReport shape: indicators n_tokens, oov_rate, team_conf (KS) and team_mix (PSI), worst wins, `insufficient_data` under 200.
  Not yet run against B2's real `reference.json` (tests use synthetic data).
