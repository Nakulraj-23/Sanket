## Choices to know
- `clean` applies NFKC first, then redacts, then collapses whitespace and lower-cases (placeholders stay uppercase). Idempotent (tested).
- A 13-19 digit number right after order/ref/reference/tracking/invoice/txn/awb is treated as an id, not a card, even if Luhn passes.
  Without that, a random 13-digit order id passes Luhn about 1 time in 10.
- Phone: Indian 10-digit starting 6-9 (optional +91/0), and `+` international with 9-15 digits. A bare 10-digit amount starting 6-9 would be masked.
